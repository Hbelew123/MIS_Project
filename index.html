Side Quest Coffee Co. is a neighborhood coffee shop with room for an unexpectedly good afternoon. Grab a drink, settle in, and enjoy a physical board game, cards, dice, or chess.

Meeting a friend, working for a while, or learning your first tabletop game? You’re welcome here. You don’t have to be a board-game person to pull up a chair.
